# Quick Start Guide

## Running the App

```bash
npm run dev
```

Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)

## Using the App

### Step 1: Choose Your Story 📖
Select one of three storylines:
- **A Day in the Life** - Follow your child through a special day
- **Adventure Journey** - An exciting quest story
- **Friendship Story** - A heartwarming friendship tale

### Step 2: Fill in the Details ✍️
Complete the form fields for your chosen storyline:
- Character names
- Settings and locations
- Emotions and themes
- Custom plot elements

### Step 3: Upload Pictures 📸
- Click "Choose Images" to upload up to 10 pictures
- Images are automatically converted to line drawings
- Wait for all images to process (green checkmark appears)
- Click "Continue to Preview"

### Step 4: Download Your Book 📥
- Review all 10 pages with your images and story
- Edit the book title if desired
- Click "Download PDF" to save your book
- Print or share with family!

## Example Workflow

1. **Choose "A Day in the Life"**
2. **Fill in:**
   - Child's Name: Emma
   - Age: 4
   - Main Activity: Playing at the park
   - Time of Day: Sunny afternoon
   - Life Lesson: Kindness

3. **Upload 3-4 photos:**
   - Photo of your child
   - Family photo
   - Park/outdoor photo
   - Any other meaningful image

4. **Preview the book:**
   - 10 pages generated automatically
   - Each page has your uploaded image + story text
   - Title: "My Story Book" (editable)

5. **Download PDF:**
   - Print quality PDF file
   - Ready for printing at home or professional print shop

## Tips

- **Best Image Types**: Outdoor photos, family pictures, activity moments
- **Image Quality**: Clear, well-lit photos work best for line drawing conversion
- **More Images**: Upload more than needed - the app will cycle through them across the 10 pages
- **Custom Details**: The more specific your details, the more personalized the story!
- **Printing**: 8.5" x 11" landscape format is perfect for board books

## File Output

The PDF will be named after your book title (e.g., "My Story Book.pdf")

### Print Options:
- **Home Printing**: Use standard color printer with good quality paper
- **Professional Printing**: Upload to print-on-demand services like Lulu, Blurb, or Amazon KDP
- **Board Book Format**: Standard 8.5" x 11" landscape - ask your local print shop for board book binding options

## Troubleshooting

**Images not processing?**
- Check file size (should be under 10MB)
- Ensure image format is supported (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF)
- Try refreshing the page

**PDF won't download?**
- Check browser download settings
- Try a different browser
- Ensure pop-ups aren't blocked

**Story text looks cut off?**
- This is normal for the preview - PDF will have proper text wrapping
- Download and check the PDF file directly
